WHAT WE OFFER

What does Brazil Translations offer?

Brazil Translations specializes in converting written or spoken content from one language to another while ensuring that the meaning, tone, and context are fully preserved.  Our services cover written translations, oral interpretation, and cultural content adaptation.

What types of translation does Brazil Translations perform?
  • Translation of documents:   From legal, technical, and academic to literary and commercial texts, we handle a wide range of content.  

  • Sworn or certified translation: Officially certified translations by a sworn public translator, typically required for legal or governmental purposes. 

  • Simultaneous & consecutive interpretation: Real-time oral translations for events, conferences, and meetings.

  • Software and website localization:   Adaptation of systems or websites to meet the linguistic and cultural expectations of different markets. 

How is translation accuracy guaranteed?

We ensure precision through the use of experienced, specialized translators, a meticulous review process by qualified professionals, and advanced tools such as translation memory and comprehensive glossaries, which maintain consistency in terminology across all projects.

What is the profile of the translators?

Our translators are highly qualified professionals fluent in two or more languages, with specialized training in fields such as law, medicine, technology, and business, being skilled to navigate the cultural nuances of each language.

How long does it take to translate a document?

The delivery deadline is set depending on the volume and complexity of the content.  On average, a translator can produce 4,000 to 5,000 words per day.  More specialized or technical texts may require additional time for accurate and thorough translation.

What languages does Brazil Translations offer?

We provide expert translation services in over 60 languages. To learn more about the specific languages we cover, please reach out to one of our consultants for detailed information.

How are translation service prices calculated?

Our pricing is based on the number of words or pages (160 words per page) in the original document.

Is it possible to translate technical or specialized materials?

Yes, we specialize in translating highly technical and specialized materials, including areas such as medicine, engineering, law, IT, marketing, and more, ensuring that industry-specific terminology is accurately conveyed.

Why hire a translation company instead of freelance translators?

Brazil Translations provides a team of experienced specialists, rigorous quality control processes, guaranteed deadlines, and access to a vast network of professional translators, ensuring consistent, reliable, and high-quality services.

How does document confidentiality work?

We adhere to strict confidentiality protocols to protect your sensitive information.  All our translators sign non-disclosure agreements (NDA’s), and we utilize secure systems for the transmission and storage of sensitive documents.
